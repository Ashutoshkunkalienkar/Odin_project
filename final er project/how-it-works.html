How It Works

Simple, secure, and automated tenant verification in minutes. Get comprehensive insights with just a few clicks.

1

Create Account

Sign up for an account and choose a verification plan that suits your needs. Get started in minutes.

2

Invite Tenant

Send a verification request to your prospective tenant via email or SMS. They'll receive a secure link.

3

Upload Documents

Tenant uploads required documents including ID, proof of income, and previous landlord references.

4

AI Analysis

Our AI system automatically verifies document authenticity and analyzes all provided information.

5

Background Check

Comprehensive checks against criminal databases, credit bureaus, and tenant blacklists.

6

Get Results

Receive a detailed risk assessment report with recommendations within minutes.

7

Make Decision

Review the comprehensive report and make an informed decision about your prospective tenant.
