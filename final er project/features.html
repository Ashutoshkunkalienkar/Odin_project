Powerful Features

Comprehensive tenant verification with cutting-edge AI technology designed for modern property management.

Document Verification

AI-powered OCR and document authenticity checks for IDs, passports, and utility bills. Verify identity documents in seconds.

Face Recognition

Biometric face matching to verify tenant identity against provided documents. Advanced liveness detection prevents spoofing.

Criminal Background

Comprehensive criminal record check across national and local databases. Instant alerts for any concerning records.

Financial Analysis

Credit score evaluation and financial responsibility assessment. Analyze payment history and debt-to-income ratio.

Blacklist Check

Check against database of known problematic tenants and evictions. Protect your property from repeat offenders.

Risk Scoring

AI-generated comprehensive risk score based on all verification factors. Make data-driven decisions quickly.

Lease Analysis

Automated lease document review and validation. Ensure compliance with local regulations and standards.

Rental History

Verify previous landlord references and rental history. Check for any past lease violations or disputes.

Real-time Alerts

Instant notifications for any changes in tenant status or new records found in ongoing monitoring.
